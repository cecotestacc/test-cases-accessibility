Shared Components Test Set — Summary

Folder: shared-components/  ·  20 pages  ·  Lumino fictional e-commerce site  ·  Created 2025-09-29
TL;DR: 20 HTML pages simulating a fictional e-commerce site ("Lumino"). 15 pages carry #marquee-message with a deliberate WCAG 1.4.12 text-spacing violation (fixed height + overflow:hidden with tight line-height). All 20 pages carry a .subscribe-btn with a deliberate WCAG 1.4.3 colour-contrast violation. Together, these produce two predictable shared-component groups for verifying QualiBooth's shared-element grouping feature.

Scope & Purpose

This test set was created to validate QualiBooth's shared components feature — the portal capability that groups accessibility issues by CSS selector across all pages in a scan run, showing per-selector page counts, issue counts, issue types, and share-of-run percentages. The pages are realistic enough to resemble a real site crawl while keeping violation counts fully predictable for regression testing.

Violations Engineered

Violation 1: #marquee-message — Text Spacing Overlap WCAG 1.4.12 AA 15 pages

Mechanism: The #marquee-message element has height: 40px; overflow: hidden; padding: 4px 20px 0; font-size: 14px;. Inside it, two .marquee-line spans are rendered as blocks with line-height: 1.2.

Normal state: Two lines × (14px × 1.2 line-height) = 33.6px content + 4px top padding = 37.6px total scrollHeight < 40px clientHeight → no overflow.

After text-spacing override (axe-core applies line-height: 1.5em !important): Two lines × (14px × 1.5) = 42px + 4px = 46px scrollHeight > 40px clientHeight → content clipped → violation reported on #marquee-message.

Expected: 1 violation per page · 15 total violations across 15 pages · Selector: #marquee-message · Rule: text-spacing (axe-core)
Violation 2: .subscribe-btn — Colour Contrast WCAG 1.4.3 AA 20 pages (all)

Mechanism: Footer <button class="subscribe-btn"> has color: #ffffff (white text) on background: #e2e8f0 (very light gray). Calculated contrast ratio ≈ 1.22:1 — well below the 4.5:1 required for normal text.

This violation appears on all 20 pages, providing the "other issues" needed to make the #marquee-message share-of-run percentage non-trivial and testable.

Expected: 1 violation per page · 20 total violations · Selector: likely .subscribe-btn · Rule: color-contrast (axe-core)

Design Decisions

  • Realistic page content — pages simulate a genuine e-commerce site ("Lumino") so the scan resembles a real crawl rather than a synthetic test harness.
  • Intentional exclusions — FAQ, Careers, Privacy, Terms, and Sitemap pages deliberately omit #marquee-message, simulating the real-world pattern where announcement banners appear only on marketing/content pages.
  • Two distinct violation types — text-spacing on #marquee-message and colour-contrast on .subscribe-btn produce two independent shared-component groups, enabling full regression coverage of the grouping feature.
  • No unintended violations — all other colours, contrast ratios, alt attributes, ARIA labels, and heading structure are verified to meet WCAG AA so that only the two engineered violations appear in scan results.
  • No QA panel overlay — unlike behaviour-feature/ test cases, these pages omit the QA panel to keep the DOM clean and avoid introducing additional axe-core findings.

Sustainability at Lumino 2025

Our annual sustainability report is here. Here's what we've achieved, where we've fallen short, and what we're committing to next.

By Lumino Sustainability Team · 22 May 2025 · 6 min read

Packaging Progress

We reached 80% recycled packaging across all orders this quarter, up from 61% at the same point last year. Our tissue paper, mailers, and shipping boxes are now all made from post-consumer recycled materials and are fully recyclable at home.

Tree Planting

In partnership with Trees for the Future, every order placed on lumino.com contributes to tree planting in sub-Saharan Africa. We crossed the 10,000 tree milestone in May 2025 — a landmark we're proud of, and a floor we intend to build on significantly.

Supply Chain Transparency

100% of our tier-one suppliers are now publicly listed on our website, with independently verified wage and safety audits conducted twice yearly. We're working to extend this transparency to tier-two and tier-three suppliers by end of 2026.

Where We've Fallen Short

We did not meet our target of reducing carbon emissions per order by 15% this year. We achieved 8%. The shortfall stems from increased air freight during stock shortages — something we're addressing through longer lead-time planning and regional warehousing.

Commitments for 2026

100% recycled packaging, Scope 3 emissions reporting for all direct suppliers, and a take-back and resale programme for worn Lumino products. We'll report progress every six months.

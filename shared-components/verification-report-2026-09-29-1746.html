Portal Threshold Behaviour (Reference)

How the portal threshold works

The QualiBooth portal groups violations by selector. It applies two levels of threshold logic:

  • Level 1 — Component visibility: A component appears only if at least one (selector, rule_id) pair fires on 2+ pages. A union-of-pages across multiple rules is not sufficient.
  • Level 2 — Sub-issue visibility: Sub-issues with <2 pages are HIDDEN when other sub-issues for the same selector have 2+ pages; shown otherwise.

Hidden sub-issues are dimmed in the table above and marked HIDDEN 1pg. Issues and Share% columns count shown sub-issues only, matching portal behaviour.

Known Uncertainties & Scan-Depth Variations

Behavioral-only detections may be absent in axe-core-only scan

Four components require QualiBooth behavioral scanning to be fully detected: .hero-section (gradient contrast), .tooltip-btn (hover/focus 3 sub-issues), #product-modal (modal lifecycle), and nav.site-nav (focus not obscured). If the scan runs axe-core only:

  • .hero-section — may not appear at all
  • .tooltip-btn — shows 1 issue type (ARIA) instead of 4; 8 issues instead of 32
  • #product-modal — may not appear
  • nav.site-nav — shows 1 issue type (landmark-unique) instead of 2; 25 issues instead of 50
Document which mode was used for each scan run. Compare behavioral results against behavioral baseline, and axe-core results against axe-core baseline separately.
Selector precision — axe-core may generate a more specific selector

axe-core generates CSS selectors automatically. .cookie-btn might be reported as button.cookie-btn or #cookie-banner > button.cookie-btn. Similarly .modal-close might be .modal-box > button.modal-close. If a component appears under a more specific selector, update the baseline — the page count and issue count are still the ground truth.

Pages 21–25 (support) — verify they appear in run total but NOT in most component lists

account, orders, faq, careers, and store-locator should appear in the scan run total (25 pages) but should NOT appear in the pages lists for promo-bar, cookie-btn, hero-section, tooltip-btn, toast-region, product-modal, section-title, or ghost-cta.

They SHOULD appear in the pages lists for meta[name="viewport"], nav.site-nav, and nav.footer-nav.

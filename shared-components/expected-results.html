Known Uncertainties

Selector for .subscribe-btn may vary

axe-core generates selectors automatically. For <button class="subscribe-btn">, the generated selector may be .subscribe-btn, button.subscribe-btn, .footer-inner > button, or something more specific depending on axe-core's selector algorithm version.

If the portal shows the button under a different selector, update the expected selector in this document and record the actual selector as the regression baseline.
axe-core may report violations on child elements inside #marquee-message

If axe-core reports the text-spacing violation on .marquee-line (the child spans) rather than on #marquee-message itself, the shared components group would appear under a different selector. The violation count per page might be 2 (one per span) rather than 1.

If this occurs: update expected issue count to 30 (2 per page × 15 pages), update selector, and re-calculate share percentages: 30/(30+20) = 60%.
Confirm: pages WITHOUT #marquee-message are NOT listed under the component

The five pages without #marquee-message (faq, careers, privacy, terms, sitemap) must not appear in the pages list under the #marquee-message component. Verify that the portal shows exactly 15 pages, not 20.

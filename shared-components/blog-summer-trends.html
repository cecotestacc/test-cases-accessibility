Summer Fashion Trends 2025

From runway to real life — the five trends dominating summer 2025 and how to wear them without the effort.

By Lumino Editorial · 14 June 2025 · 4 min read

1. The Return of Pastels

Soft lavender, dusty rose, and sage green have taken over runways from Milan to New York. Unlike the washed-out palettes of previous summers, this season's pastels come saturated enough to make a statement while staying effortlessly wearable.

Pair a sage linen shirt with off-white wide-leg trousers for a look that reads polished without feeling overdressed.

2. Oversized Silhouettes

The relaxed, oversized aesthetic shows no signs of slowing. Boxy blazers, drop-shoulder tees, and wide-leg cuts are all fair game. The key to making it work: balance volume on top with a slimmer bottom, or vice versa.

3. Retro Print Revival

Paisley, abstract geometrics, and classic bandana prints are back — but translated into modern, wearable cuts. A retro-print camp-collar shirt with clean chino shorts is the shorthand for effortless summer style.

4. Utility Details

Cargo pockets, D-ring hardware, and belted waists are crossing over from workwear into fashion. Look for utility details done in lighter fabrics and cleaner silhouettes to keep things seasonal.

5. Tonal Dressing

Head-to-toe colour matching is the quiet-luxury move of 2025. Choose one shade and build an entire outfit around it — texture and proportion do the work of contrast.

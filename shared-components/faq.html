Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us at help@lumino.com and we'll get back to you within one business day.

Orders & Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express (next day) delivery is available on orders placed before 2pm. Free delivery on all orders over $75.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be amended or cancelled within one hour of placement. After that, our warehouse begins processing and changes are no longer possible. Please contact us immediately via live chat.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship to 14 countries. Delivery times and costs vary by destination. Full details are shown at checkout before you confirm your order.

Returns & Refunds

What is your returns policy?

We offer free returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of us receiving the item.

How do I start a return?

Log into your Lumino account, go to Orders, select the item, and click Return. You'll receive a prepaid returns label by email within minutes.

Sizing

How do I find my size?

Each product page has a detailed size guide specific to that garment. If you're between sizes, our general rule is to size up for a relaxed fit or size down for a slim fit. Our virtual styling service can also help.

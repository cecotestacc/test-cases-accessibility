About Lumino

Founded in 2018, Lumino began as a small boutique with a big idea: fashion should be accessible, ethical, and effortlessly stylish for everyone.

Our Story

What started in a converted warehouse in East London has grown into a global community of over two million shoppers. We believe that what you wear reflects who you are, and we are committed to helping you express that authentically — without breaking the bank.

Our founders, Mia and James Chen, spent five years working in fast fashion before deciding there had to be a better way. They launched Lumino with ten styles, a small team, and a commitment to transparency that remains at the heart of everything we do.

Our Values

We hold ourselves to three principles: quality over quantity, transparency over spin, and community over growth-at-any-cost. Every decision — from sourcing to packaging — runs through this filter.

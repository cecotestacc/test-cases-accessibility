Portal Threshold Behaviour

How the portal threshold works

Level 1 (component appears): A component appears only if at least one (selector, rule_id) pair fires on 2+ pages. If only a union of different rules reaches 2 pages but no single rule does, the component is excluded.

Level 2 (sub-issues shown): Once a component appears, sub-issues on only 1 page are hidden when at least one sibling sub-issue has 2+ pages. Issues and Share% exclude hidden sub-issues.

In this table, hidden sub-issues are listed but visually dimmed and marked HIDDEN 1pg. Issues and Share% count shown sub-issues only.

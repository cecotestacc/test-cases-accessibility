Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025. This policy explains how Lumino Ltd collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect data you provide directly (name, email, delivery address, payment details) and data generated automatically when you visit our site (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent). We do not sell your data to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to fulfil orders, personalise your experience, send marketing communications (with your consent), and improve our services. We retain order data for seven years as required by UK tax law.

3. Marketing Communications

If you opt in to our newsletter, we'll send you style updates, offers, and event invitations. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link at the bottom of every email or by contacting privacy@lumino.com.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to operate the site, analytics cookies to understand how it's used (with your consent), and marketing cookies to personalise ads (with your consent). You can manage your preferences at any time via the cookie banner.

5. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@lumino.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Contact

Data Controller: Lumino Ltd, 42 King Street, London EC2A 4BH. For privacy queries: privacy@lumino.com.

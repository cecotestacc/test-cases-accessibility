Big news: We're opening 3 new stores in Q4 this year! Stay tuned for grand opening events and exclusive launch offers near you

Q3 2025 Company Update

Three new stores, a milestone in sustainable packaging, and what's coming next for Lumino in the second half of the year.

By Lumino Communications · 2 June 2025 · 3 min read

New Store Openings

We are thrilled to announce that three new Lumino flagship stores will open in Q4 2025 — in Manchester, Edinburgh, and Dublin. Each location will carry our full product range alongside a personalised styling service and an in-store repair workshop.

Grand opening events will be announced on our social channels and via email. Sign up below to be first in the queue for launch-day invites and exclusive discounts.

Sustainability Milestone

As of June 2025, 80% of all Lumino orders ship in fully recycled and recyclable packaging. Our target is 100% by end of year, and we are on track. We also crossed the 10,000-tree mark this quarter through our partnership with Trees for the Future.

What's Coming Next

The autumn-winter collection drops in August and is our most ambitious yet — 120 styles across three colour stories, with an expanded menswear range and our first dedicated kidswear line. More details to follow in July.

Thank you for being part of the Lumino community. As always, we're building this for you.

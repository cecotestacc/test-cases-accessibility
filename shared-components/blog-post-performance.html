The Science of Performance Footwear

What makes a great running shoe? We break down the tech behind cushioning, stability, and propulsion.

Latest posts

Modern running shoes are engineering marvels. The midsole foam, the carbon-fibre plate, the outsole compound — each element is designed to return energy and protect the joints across thousands of repetitions.

At Lumino, we work directly with materials scientists to source foams that balance energy return with durability. Our Trail Runner Pro, for example, uses a dual-density midsole: firm for stability on uneven terrain, cushioned for impact absorption on landing.

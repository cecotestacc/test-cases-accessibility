Case #01 focus-obscured
WCAG criteria
2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) — AA  ·  2.4.12 Focus Not Obscured (Enhanced) — AAA
Elements with errors
<sticky-header> · <announce-banner> · <focusable-card> (cards 1 & 2)
Issues
  • ERROR 2.4.11 <sticky-header> is position:fixed; height:80px; z-index:100 — when cards near the top of the page receive focus the header layer fully paints over the focused element.
  • ERROR 2.4.11 <announce-banner> is position:sticky; top:80px; z-index:50 — creates a second opaque layer directly below the header, obscuring Card 1 when it receives focus.
  • ERROR 2.4.12 Cards 1 & 2: nine hit-test points on the focused element fall under higher-z-index layers — the element is entirely (2.4.11) or partially (2.4.12) covered.

Case #02 focus-obscured W3C F110
WCAG criteria
2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) — AA  ·  2.4.12 Focus Not Obscured (Enhanced) — AAA
W3C technique
F110: Failure of SC 2.4.11 due to a sticky header or footer obscuring focused content
Elements with errors
<header> (position:fixed; height:72px; z-index:200) · .filter-bar (position:sticky; top:72px; z-index:100) · Card 1 and Card 2 links
Issues
  • ERROR 2.4.11 <header> is position:fixed; z-index:200 — when Card 1's link receives Tab focus it sits near the top of the content area and the header fully paints over it. Nine hit-test points on the focused element all fall under the header layer.
  • ERROR 2.4.11 .filter-bar is position:sticky; top:72px; z-index:100 — creates a second opaque band immediately below the header, partially or fully covering Card 2's link when it receives focus.
  • ERROR 2.4.12 Even partial coverage by either layer causes the AAA criterion to fail for the affected cards.

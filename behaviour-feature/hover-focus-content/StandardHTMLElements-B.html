WCAG criteria
1.4.13 Content on Hover or Focus — AA
W3C technique
SCR39: Making content on focus or hover hoverable, dismissible, and persistent (shown as failure)
Elements with errors
#aria-trigger / #aria-tip
Issues
  • ERROR 1.4.13 hoverable pointer-events:none on the tooltip means the pointer cannot move from the trigger into the tip — the tip disappears the moment the cursor leaves the trigger element. The rule's "pointer moves into tooltip" test fails.
  • ERROR 1.4.13 dismissible No Escape-key handler is attached. Users who need to dismiss the tip without moving the pointer (e.g. screen-magnification users) have no mechanism to do so.

Two-Column Footer — Proper Column Gap Preserves Tab Order

The newsletter submit button fills its column exactly (width: 100%; box-sizing: border-box). A 20 px flex gap separates the newsletter column from the catalogue panel, so button.right is always 20 px to the left of catalogue.left. The geometry engine detects the column boundary and reports no tab-order issue.

Case #08 text-spacing
WCAG criteria
1.4.12 Text Spacing — AA
Elements with errors
<content-card> · <status-badge> · <action-btn> (inside <button-row>)
Issues
  • ERROR <content-card> has height:120px; overflow:hidden — when line-height is forced to 1.5× font-size (SC 1.4.12), body text is clipped by the fixed container.
  • ERROR <status-badge> has width:72px; height:24px; overflow:hidden — adding letter-spacing of 0.12em pushes "Pending" and "Closed" outside the badge bounds.
  • ERROR <button-row> uses flex-wrap:nowrap; gap:4px — word-spacing of 0.16em causes button labels to grow and visually collide or overflow the row.

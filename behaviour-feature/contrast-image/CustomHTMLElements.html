Case #06 contrast-image
WCAG criteria
1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) — AA
Elements with errors
<hero-banner> · <image-card> (both) · <video-overlay>
Issues
  • ERROR <hero-banner>: white headline (#fff) on a gradient that fades from orange to white — contrast collapses to 1:1 at the right end of the gradient.
  • ERROR <image-card> (mountain): white caption text directly over a photo — actual contrast per glyph depends on the pixels beneath it; cannot meet 4.5:1 reliably.
  • ERROR <image-card> (city): #888888 caption over a photo — mid-grey on a variable image background almost certainly falls below 4.5:1.
  • ERROR <video-overlay>: #dddddd text over a video frame — background luminance changes every frame, making a static check insufficient.

Case #03 contrast-image W3C G18 — Example B
WCAG criteria
1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) — AA (4.5:1 for normal text)
W3C technique
G18: Ensuring a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 between text and background (shown as failure — non-flat backdrop)
Elements with errors
.photo-section p (color #ffffff, normal weight)
Issues
  • ERROR White (#ffffff) normal-weight text sits on a multi-layer gradient that simulates a photograph. Over the near-white diagonal stripe regions individual glyph pixels have contrast as low as 1.2:1. The rule captures the text, makes glyphs transparent to expose the background pixel, and compares each glyph pixel against the background pixel behind it.
